01 · Why this problem

Childhood pneumonia still kills.

CXR is the cheap test. Reads are slow, variable, and scarce where the deaths are.

~2Munder-5 deaths / yr
1–5 yrKermany age band
3 labelsnormal · bacterial · viral
1 siteGuangzhou, one hospital
Kermany 2018 cites WHO: about 2 million children under 5 per year. Later papers use ~700k — an updated WHO figure. Quote the Cell number when you are talking about the source paper. The project is classification, not a deployed CAD system.

02 · The dataset · analog of APTOS

Kermany 2018 is the public set.

Released as the CXR generalization experiment in the Cell OCT paper. Became the Kaggle “Chest X-Ray Images (Pneumonia)” collection.

Cell protocol is 5,232 train and 624 patient-held-out test. Kaggle moved 16 training images into a toy val folder. The 16-image val set is not in the paper. Mention it now; we come back when 99% papers appear.

03 · Source paper · from the PDF

They froze Inception V3.

Same ImageNet Inception V3 they used for OCT. Convolutional layers locked as feature extractors. Bottlenecks cached. Only the softmax is trained.

Adam, LR 0.001, 1,000 images/step, 10,000 steps (100 epochs). GTX 1080. Patient-independent holdout after every step.

Unfreezing the conv stack and fine-tuning decreased accuracy — they blame overfitting. STAR Methods, not later folklore.

Clinical labels, not just names: bacterial = urgent referral (antibiotics); viral = supportive care; normal = observation.

This is the fact later papers blur. Kermany did not fine-tune last blocks. The 2026 bake-off on this same set finds the opposite: fine-tune +5.5%. That disagreement is now a project ablation.

Rahman Table 5 lists 90.1% as precision. The Cell text says specificity. Bacterial-versus-viral is already two points worse — the hard-class story starts on page one of this dataset. Occlusion maps in the paper are for OCT, not CXR.

06 · The common pattern

APTOS had a recipe. So does Kermany.

ImageNet CNN transferred onto Kermany. The source paper froze the backbone; later papers usually fine-tune. Accuracy + AUC + recall reported. Viral pneumonia — not the obvious consolidations — is the hard class.

07 · Backbones

What they actually trained.

From scratch

Stephen 2019 custom CNN — 93.7% val acc.
LightPneumoNet 2025 — 388k params, 94.2% acc, 0.99 recall.

Single tower, ImageNet

Kermany Inception V3, frozen. Rajaraman VGG16. Rahman DenseNet201. Salehi DenseNet121. 2026 bake-off: ResNet50 fine-tune wins.

Ensembles

Ayan, best as three models. Kundu: GoogLeNet + ResNet-18 + DenseNet-121 — the one that also leaves Kermany.

Hybrids / XAI

XCCNet dilated CNN + transformer. Grad-CAM / Score-CAM / LIME on the hybrids and the 2026 bake-off.

If asked “which backbone should we use?” — ResNet50 or DenseNet121, and ablate frozen vs last-block fine-tune. Kermany froze and said unfreezing overfit; 2026 says the opposite. Ensemble only after that baseline is honest.

08 · Class imbalance

About 3 : 1 pneumonia : normal.

Bacterial also outnumbers viral. Almost nobody uses focal loss on this set.

  • None in the source paper — Kermany: transfer learning only, no weights or CXR aug.
  • Geometric aug — rotate, shift, shear, zoom, flip (Stephen, Rahman).
  • Minority duplication — Ayan 1,349 → 2,534 normals.
  • Balance-to-N — Rahman augments every class to 4,500.
  • SMOTE — Salehi (the odd one out).
  • Class weights — LightPneumoNet 2.0 / 1.2.
  • GAN / ADA synthetics — XCCNet manufactures extra normals.
Recommend class weights + light aug for our project. GAN synthesis is extra scope.

09 · Preprocessing

From “resize and hope” to a CXR stack.

Default 2018–21

224×224 or 227×227. 1/255 or ImageNet mean/std. Duplicate the gray channel to RGB so the pretrained stem accepts it.

What actually moved the needle

Lung ROI at 1024² (Rajaraman). CLAHE, lung seg, rib suppression (XCCNet). Grayscale on (LightPneumoNet). Flip off, because left/right is anatomy.

A semester-scale ablation: full film vs cropped lung, with and without CLAHE, is more original than a sixth ensemble.

10 · Metrics

Not quadratic weighted kappa.

Kermany is not an ordinal 5-grade scale. Papers report:

Accuracy Sensitivity / recall Specificity Precision F1 AUC

Binary accuracy is the vanity number. Three-class accuracy and viral recall are the honest ones. Screening papers chase recall and leak false positives.

If a reviewer asks why we are not using QWK: because the labels are nominal (normal/bacterial/viral), not ordered severity. Macro-F1 plus per-class recall is the right analog.

11 · Foundational three

2018–19: the set, the crop, the scratch net.

Kermany · Cell · 2018

Frozen Inception V3. Pneumonia vs normal 92.8% acc, 93.2% sens, 90.1% spec, 96.8% AUC. Bacterial vs viral 90.7%. Created the public set.

Rajaraman · Appl. Sci. · 2018

Custom VGG16 on lung ROI. 96.2% disease / 93.6% bacterial–viral. First serious CAM/LIME paper here.

Stephen · JHE · 2019

Scratch CNN + heavy Keras aug. 93.73% val acc. Custom 3722/2134 split — not the official test.

Lesson

These three are fully open and still define the dataset, the lung-crop idea, and the from-scratch baseline.

12 · 2020–21

The useful mid-period papers.

Rahman · 2020

DenseNet201 on three task cuts: 98% binary, 93.3% three-class, 95% bacterial vs viral. The clean comparison table.

Salehi · BJR · 2021

DenseNet121 + SMOTE. 86.8% acc, AUC 0.86. Radiology journal. Warns about patient leakage.

Ayan et al. · 2021

Ensemble 95.83% binary, 90.71% three-class. Viral recall 77% vs bacterial 98%.

Kundu et al. · 2021

GoogLeNet + ResNet-18 + DenseNet-121. 98.81% on Kermany, 86.95% on RSNA. The generalization slide.

Salehi next to Rahman is the 86 vs 98 contrast. Ayan is the viral-class slide. Kundu is the “it does not travel” slide. Extra five-model ensembles were cut.

13 · 2024–26

Hybrids, small nets, honest ablations.

XCCNet · 2024

Dilated CNN + contrastive transformer. CLAHE, lung seg, rib suppress, GAN normals. 99.76% on a balanced 80/10/10. Four datasets.

LightPneumoNet · 2025

388k-param scratch CNN, class weights, grayscale. 94.2% acc, 0.99 recall. The deployable counter-argument.

TL vs scratch · 2026

ResNet50 fine-tune 99.43% on a reshuffled 523-image test. Frozen backbones lag by ~5.5 points. Grad-CAM on every error type.

Lesson

99% is a split story. The useful experiment is freeze vs fine-tune on the Cell 624 test, with Grad-CAM on the errors.

15 · Consensus

What the ten papers agree on.

  • Transfer an ImageNet CNN. Whether to freeze it is not settled — Kermany froze; 2026 unfreezes and gains ~5 points.
  • DenseNet and ResNet are enough. EfficientNet is not automatically better here.
  • Augment or reweight the normal (and viral) class. Accuracy alone lies.
  • Viral vs bacterial is the remaining clinical task. Already 2 points worse in the Cell paper.
  • Kermany does not travel — expect a double-digit drop on RSNA or another hospital.

16 · Open fights

Where they do not agree.

Is 99% pneumonia, or Guangzhou film-processor texture?

Freeze the backbone (Kermany) vs fine-tune last blocks (2026, +5.5%).

One well-tuned ResNet vs a three-model ensemble (Ayan, Kundu).

Cell 624 test vs Kaggle’s 16-image val vs a homemade 80/10/10. This is the real leaderboard bug.

17 · For our project

A baseline that would survive a viva.

  1. State the split. Cell 624 or patient-level 80/10/10. Never both quietly.
  2. Train ResNet50 (or DenseNet121). Ablate frozen vs last-block fine-tune.
  3. Report both Cell tasks: pneumonia-vs-normal and bacterial-vs-viral. Lead with viral recall.
  4. Scratch CNN as the control (Stephen / LightPneumoNet).
  5. Grad-CAM on errors, aimed at Figure S6 signs — focal consolidation vs interstitial pattern.
This is the slide to leave up in Q&A. If they ask for novelty: freeze vs fine-tune on the official 624, lung-crop ablation, or viral-class loss.
